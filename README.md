# 🌬️ Breathly - Quick Installation Guide

Welcome to **Breathly**, your controlled breathing and cardiac coherence companion, designed to work anywhere, anytime, even completely offline (Airplane Mode).

To install Breathly as a standalone app on your device, follow the simple instructions below.

---

## 📱 Installation on Smartphones & Tablets

### 🍏 On iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open the app link in the **Safari** browser.
2. Wait on the page for **10 seconds** without touching anything (this allows the background audio assets to load into cache).
3. Tap the **Share** button 📤 (the square icon with an upward arrow at the bottom of your screen).
4. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen** ➕.
5. Keep the name "Breathly" and confirm.
6. **Ultimate test:** Close Safari, launch the app from your home screen, turn on **Airplane Mode**, and take a deep breath!

### 🤖 On Android (Chrome)
1. Open the app link in the **Google Chrome** browser.
2. Wait on the page for **10 seconds**.
3. Tap the **3 dots menu** 💬 in the top right corner.
4. Select **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**.
5. **Ultimate test:** Close Chrome, launch the app from your home screen, switch to **Airplane Mode**, and enjoy!

---

## 💻 Installation on Computers (PC / Mac)
1. Open the link in **Chrome**, **Edge**, or **Brave**.
2. On the right side of the URL address bar, click the small monitor icon with an arrow (or the **"+"** icon).
3. Click **Install**. The app will now open in its own clean, borderless window.

---
⚠️ *Note: For future updates, simply open the app once while connected to the internet. It will update itself automatically in the background.*
